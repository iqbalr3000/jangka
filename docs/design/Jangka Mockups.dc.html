Refund self-service

Owner Ilham R. · Updated 2h ago · Upstream↑ BR-002↑ 🔒 Restricted document

Overview

Let customers request and track refunds from the order detail page without contacting support. Covers card and wallet payments in the ID and MY markets.

Problem Statement

Customers wait 5–7 days for refunds and have no visibility into status. Refund status questions are the largest single category of support tickets, and CS agents process every refund by hand in the payments console.

Goals & Success Metrics

◐ Partial ⟳ ReviseHistoryCopy link
┊ Success metric has no target.
  • Customers see refund status in-app at every step.
  • Refunds under $50 are approved without an agent.
  • Reduce refund-related support tickets.
